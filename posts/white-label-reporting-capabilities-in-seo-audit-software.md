## What White-Label Reporting Encompasses

The term covers several distinct capabilities that platforms implement unevenly. At minimum, white-label reporting means replacing the platform's logo with an agency logo in exported PDF reports. At the more capable end, it means a fully branded client portal where the client can log in, view their site's current audit state, review historical trend data, and download exports â€” all without encountering the platform's name, domain, or branding anywhere in the interface.

Between these poles are intermediate implementations: branded PDF exports with custom color schemes, report header customization with agency contact details, and custom subdomain hosting for the reporting interface (e.g., `reports.agencyname.com`). Each level of white-labeling carries different value depending on the client relationship context.

## Evaluating for Agency Use
